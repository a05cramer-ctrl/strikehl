How it works

One pool wallet, four memos, no custody of anything but the stake you send.

Pick

Any Hyperliquid perp doing $1M+ in 24h volume. BTC, ETH, SOL, HYPE and the rest. CALL if you think it's higher in an hour, PUT if lower.

Send

One Phantom transaction: your SOL stake to the pool, with the memo STRIKE|CALL|BTC.

Lock

The pool sees it within ~10 seconds, reads the Hyperliquid mid price and writes your strike and expiry on-chain.

Settle

60 minutes later it reads the price again. Right side of the strike: 1.8x your stake lands in your wallet. Wrong side: the stake stays in the pool.
